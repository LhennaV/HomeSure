Frequently Asked Questions 6 Articles
How long does identity verification take?
Verification submissions are manually reviewed by HomeSure admins within 24 to 48 hours. Once approved, your account badge turns green and you receive instant access to direct seller messaging and property reservations.
Is my payment and security deposit protected?
Yes! All online transactions on HomeSure are processed securely through certified payment gateways (Xendit) supporting GCash, Maya, and credit/debit cards. Deposits and reservations are held in escrow under verified contractual terms.
Can I schedule an in-person site viewing before reserving?
Absolutely! Open any verified listing and click "Schedule Viewing" or send a message to the seller with your preferred date and time.
How does HomeSure guarantee property title authenticity?
Every listing submitted by a seller must upload government-issued land titles (TCT/CCT), tax declarations, and notarized MOA agreements. Admins perform optical OCR and cross-checks with local registry records in Sta. Maria, Bulacan.
What should I do if a seller is unresponsive?
If a seller hasn't responded within 72 hours, you can click "Report Listing" or email our support desk. Listings with inactive sellers are automatically reviewed and deprioritized in search results.
How do I update notification preferences or enable 2FA?
Visit Settings to toggle Two-Factor Authentication, SMS notifications, email alerts, and theme preferences.
