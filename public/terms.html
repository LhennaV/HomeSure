06 · Property Listings and Authenticity

Property Listings and Authenticity

6.1 Listing Requirements

Verified sellers who post listings on HomeSure agree that all of the following conditions are met for every listing submitted:

  • The property described in the listing physically exists at the stated location within Sta. Maria, Bulacan
  • The seller holds legal ownership of the property or has express written authorization from the registered owner to list it for sale or rent
  • All property details — including address, type, price, unit size, available amenities, and photos — are accurate and not misleading
  • All photographs submitted are actual photos of the listed property. Stock images, photos of different properties, or digitally manipulated images are strictly prohibited
  • The listed price reflects the true and current asking price. Intentionally misleading pricing (i.e., advertising a price lower than what will actually be charged) is a violation of these Terms
  • Listings will be promptly updated or removed once the property has been sold, rented, or taken off the market

6.2 Required Documentation by Listing Type

The following property documents are required at the time of listing submission for admin review:

  • For Sale Listings: Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) or Original Certificate of Title (OCT), current Real Property Tax Declaration, and a valid Tax Clearance or Tax Receipt
  • For Rent / Lease Listings: Proof of ownership (TCT or OCT) or a notarized authority-to-lease agreement signed by the registered property owner
  • For Condominiums: Condominium Certificate of Title (CCT) and the latest Homeowners Association (HOA) Clearance

6.3 Listing Review Process

All newly submitted listings undergo a review by HomeSure administrators before they are published and made visible to buyers. During review, admins may:

  1. Approve the listing and make it publicly visible
  2. Request additional documentation or corrections from the seller
  3. Reject the listing if it violates these Terms, contains inaccurate information, or raises authenticity concerns

Approved listings may still be removed at any time if subsequent reports or investigations reveal violations of these Terms.

HomeSure does not guarantee the legal title, physical condition, or fitness for purpose of any listed property. Buyers are strongly advised to conduct independent due diligence and consult a licensed real estate broker or legal professional before entering any transaction.

07 · Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited Conduct

The following activities are strictly prohibited on HomeSure and may result in immediate account suspension, permanent ban, and referral to law enforcement authorities. You agree not to engage in any of the following:

7.1 Fraudulent Listings and Misrepresentation

  • Posting listings for properties that do not exist ("ghost listings") or are not available for sale or rent
  • Listing a property that you do not own, have no legal authority to list, or that is already under an active exclusive contract with another agent or platform
  • Submitting falsified, edited, borrowed, or stolen property documents (TCT, deed, tax declaration, etc.)
  • Using stock photos, AI-generated images, or photographs of a different property to represent a listing
  • Advertising a deliberately lower price to attract inquiries, then demanding a higher amount upon contact (bait-and-switch tactics)
  • Creating duplicate listings for the same property across multiple accounts or listings
  • Misrepresenting the location, size, condition, or ownership status of any listed property

7.2 Identity Fraud and Impersonation

  • Submitting falsified, altered, or another person's government-issued ID or selfie during the verification process
  • Impersonating another individual, real estate professional, company, or government official

7.3 Financial Scams and Unauthorized Transactions

  • Requesting advance payments, reservation fees, or any form of deposit through channels outside the agreed and transparent means (e.g., unofficial GCash, Palawan Express, or wire transfers without proper documentation)
  • Soliciting money or personal financial information from buyers under false pretenses
  • Using the messaging system to conduct phishing, smishing, or any type of financial fraud
  • Advertising properties at unrealistically low prices as a mechanism to collect personal data or payments from unsuspecting users

7.4 Harassment and Abuse

  • Sending threatening, harassing, abusive, discriminatory, or sexually explicit messages to other users
  • Using the platform to stalk, intimidate, or make another user feel unsafe
  • Submitting false or malicious reports against other users in bad faith

  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to any account, database, or system component of the Platform

08 · Reporting Fraudulent Activity

Reporting Fraudulent Activity

HomeSure provides multiple channels for users to report suspected fraudulent listings, scam behavior, and policy violations. We take every report seriously and investigate all valid complaints promptly.

8.1 How to Report

You may submit a report through any of the following methods:

  • In-Platform Report Button: Click the "Report" or flag icon on any listing or message thread that you believe is fraudulent, misleading, or violates these Terms
  • Email: Send a detailed report to support@homesure.com with the subject line "Fraud Report" and include the listing ID, screenshots, and a description of the issue
  • Admin Review Queue: Reports submitted in-platform are automatically routed to our moderation team for review

8.2 What to Include in Your Report

To help us investigate effectively, please include as much of the following as possible:

  • The listing ID, URL, or seller's username associated with the suspected violation
  • A clear description of the suspicious activity or why you believe the listing or user is fraudulent
  • Screenshots, message logs, or other evidence relevant to the report
  • Your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you

8.3 Investigation Process

  1. 1
    Report Received: Your report is logged and assigned to a HomeSure moderator within 24 hours.
  2. 2
    Initial Review: The moderator reviews the report, examines the listing or account in question, and may contact the reported user for a response.
  3. 3
    Action Taken: If the violation is confirmed, the listing is removed or the account is suspended within 48 hours. For serious offenses, the matter may be escalated and reported to authorities.
  4. 4
    Reporter Notification: We will notify you of the outcome of your report when possible, subject to privacy considerations.

8.4 Reporter Protection and Report Abuse

HomeSure protects users who submit good-faith reports from retaliation. However, knowingly submitting false or malicious reports intended to harm another user's account or listings is itself a violation of these Terms and may result in the reporter's account being penalized.
